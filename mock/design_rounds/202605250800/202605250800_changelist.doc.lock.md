## CHANGE: doc section `### DiagnosticCounts` ADDED to viola-core DESIGN.md.tmpl
File: `mock/crates/viola-core/DESIGN.md.tmpl`
Verification: `grep -q "^### \`DiagnosticCounts\`" mock/crates/viola-core/DESIGN.md.tmpl`.

New Resource. **Single field**: `counts: UnsafeCell<[arvo::USize; MAX_LINTS]>`. No sibling `populated` counter; a shared scalar under the parallel RunLint cohort would be a data race on Cell read-modify-write regardless of end-state commutativity. The 32-entry scan via `any_populated` at read time replaces the counter and lives entirely on the EmitDiagnostics read side, where no concurrent writer can race (phase separation past the RunLint cohort). Disjoint per-L slot writes commute trivially under the COMMUTATIVE flag. Same four-invariant Sync contract but with the RunLint cohort as multi-writer (each writing distinct slots) instead of a host-shim sole producer.
