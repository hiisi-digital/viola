`grep -q "staged: core::cell::Cell<arvo::USize>" mock/crates/viola-core/src/wus/mod.rs && ! grep -q "pub staged" mock/crates/viola-core/src/wus/mod.rs`.

Slice 1 declared `pub struct DiagnosticSink;`. Slice 7a flips to
`pub struct DiagnosticSink { staged: core::cell::Cell<arvo::USize> }` with
**private** `staged` field (not `pub`). The field exists so the type is non-ZST;
the field is not a stable surface and Slice 7b replaces it with
`writer: UnsafeCell<W>` in the generic-carrier flip. Keeping the field non-`pub`
between 7a and 7b avoids creating a backwards-compat surface that becomes a
deletion victim.

directly.
