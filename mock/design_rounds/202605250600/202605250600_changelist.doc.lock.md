## CHANGE: doc section `### MAX_DIAGS_PER_LINT` ADDED to viola-core DESIGN.md.tmpl
File: `mock/crates/viola-core/DESIGN.md.tmpl`
Verification: `grep -q "^### \`MAX_DIAGS_PER_LINT\`" mock/crates/viola-core/DESIGN.md.tmpl`.

Const cap for per-RunLint-slot output. Workspace-default `64`. Each RunLint<L> writes to `[L * MAX_DIAGS_PER_LINT, (L + 1) * MAX_DIAGS_PER_LINT)` of Column<WuDiagnostic>; ranges disjoint by construction so the COMMUTATIVE flag's parallel-write claim holds. Consumers of `Column<WuDiagnostic>` MUST size it to at least `MAX_LINTS * MAX_DIAGS_PER_LINT` slots.
