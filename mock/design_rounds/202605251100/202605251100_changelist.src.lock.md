New file. `pub struct StderrEmitter;` ZST. `impl viola_core::wus::EmitWriter for StderrEmitter` with three unit-return methods. Internal helper `write_all_to_stderr(bytes: &[u8])` loops `libc::write(2, ptr, len)` until the buffer drains; on `-1` (errno) or zero-progress retry exhaustion it panics (routes to libc::abort via the existing panic_handler in main.rs). `write_str` calls the helper on `s.as_bytes()`. `write_bytes` calls the helper directly. `flush` is a no-op (unbuffered emitter).
