# Why this round exists

The Slice 8 scoping target is "viola-cli host-shim rewire" with two distinct concerns: (1) tear down viola-core's pre-#254 in-process pipeline surface (pipeline::run, Session<N>, the legacy CaptureSink shape, RunScope as an internal carrier struct), and (2) build viola-cli's scheduler-driven host wiring on top of the new viola-core. The two concerns are atomic at the architectural level but separable at the PR level: (1) is mechanical deletion + stub work, low-risk, mostly grep-and-replace; (2) is substantive constructive work that designs the Resource-registration ceremony, the const-L RunLint registration macro, the concrete EmitWriter impl, and the scheduler-builder composition. Splitting honestly stages the transition and produces two reviewable PRs instead of one ~1500-line PR.

Precedent: Slices 5, 6, 7 all split a/b on the same logic (a is design + scaffolding + smaller-scope source, b is the substantive body). This continues that pattern.

# What lands in Slice 8a

In `viola-core/src/`:

- **Delete `pipeline.rs` entirely.** The `viola_core::pipeline::run` function is the pre-#254 in-process orchestrator. Slice 8b's scheduler-driven flow replaces it; no transitional shim per `no-legacy-shims-pre-1.0`.
- **Delete `session.rs` entirely.** The `Session<N>` LIFO container served as a manual reverse-insertion-order shutdown helper. The hilavitkutin Scheduler's `Drop` impl handles shutdown ordering for the post-#254 shape; no parallel container needed.
- **Edit `aggregate.rs`**: keep `sort_diagnostics` and `cmp_diag` (Slice 7b's EmitDiagnostics body has its own host-side sort, but `sort_diagnostics` may still be useful at the host-shim level for sibling consumers, and removing it is a separable cleanup); delete `CaptureSink` if it lives there. If `aggregate.rs` becomes a one-function module after the cleanup, leave it; the module-level docstring documents the surviving scope.
- **Edit `lib.rs`**: drop `pub mod pipeline;`, `pub mod session;`, `pub use session::Session;`. The re-exports of `Extension`, `ExtensionHost`, `ExtensionMeta` etc from `hilavitkutin_extensions` stay (they are the FFI-side surface; viola-cli still uses them for plugin loading). Drop the rustdoc bullet that documents `pipeline` and `session`.
- **Resolve ExtensionHost name collision**: `viola_core::resources::ExtensionHost` (Slice 3) and the re-exported `hilavitkutin_extensions::ExtensionHost` (lib.rs:38-44) currently coexist via path disambiguation. Slice 8a does not flip the re-export (viola-cli still needs the hilavitkutin one in stubbed form); Slice 8b chooses whether to drop the re-export entirely (relying on consumers to import from `hilavitkutin_extensions` directly) or rename the re-export to something like `HilaExtensionHost` to break the collision. The collision is documented in a BACKLOG entry for Slice 8b to settle.

In `viola-core/tests/`:

- **Delete `pipeline_e2e.rs`.** It calls `pipeline::run` and constructs `RunScope` + `CaptureSink`; the test surface goes away with the function. Slice 8b's runtime e2e test waits on hilavitkutin's `Scheduler::run` body landing (currently `unimplemented!()` per `hilavitkutin/mock/crates/hilavitkutin/src/scheduler/mod.rs` Pass 6 contract); a new e2e test that exercises the scheduler-driven path lands as a separate slice once the engine is executable.

In `viola-cli/src/main.rs`:

- **Replace `run_v1` and `run_v2` bodies with `unimplemented!("Slice 8b ships viola-cli scheduler rewire")`** at the top of each. The functions stay declared (preserves the binary's libc entry-point shape; `cargo check -p viola-cli` succeeds; `cargo build -p viola-cli` succeeds; running the binary hits the panic). Drop the pre-#254 imports that the deletions invalidate: `pipeline::run`, `Session`, `CaptureSink` (the local one in main.rs lines 854-1159 also goes; it depended on `Diagnostic` aggregation that the new shape moves into Slice 7b's WuDiagnostic).
- **Keep the binary's libc entry point intact.** main / panic handler / rust_eh_personality stay. The `--version` path and arg parsing continue to work; only the actual lint-running paths panic with the documented `unimplemented!`.
- **Drop the local CaptureSink struct + impls (lines 854-end)** since they referenced the pre-#254 Diagnostic carrier. The replacement lives in viola-cli/src/emit.rs (Slice 8b) as the concrete EmitWriter impl.

Two BACKLOG entries for Slice 8b:

1. **viola-cli scheduler wiring + concrete EmitWriter impl.** Names the full construction surface: Scheduler::builder(), .with(Resource::new(T)) for every Resource (ExtensionHost, Workspace, CiState, DiscoveredFilePaths, FileEntryBuffer, LintSlots, LintConfigBuffer, DiagnosticCounts, DiagnosticSink<W>), .with(Column::<T>::new()) for every Column, .with(WU) for every WU (LoadConfig, LoadPlugins, DiscoverFiles, RunRunner, EmitDiagnostics<W>, plus the const-L RunLint<0..MAX_LINTS-1> chain via a local macro), .build(), .run(). The const-L registration mechanism is the load-bearing design question for Slice 8b: hilavitkutin does not ship a tuple-list helper for const-generic-WU chains; viola-cli writes a local `register_lints!` macro that unrolls into 32 `.with(RunLint::<N>::new())` calls.

2. **ExtensionHost re-export disambiguation.** Slice 8b settles whether `viola_core::ExtensionHost` (re-exported from hilavitkutin_extensions) keeps the name, gets renamed, or drops entirely in favour of consumers importing the substrate type directly. Until then the collision with `viola_core::resources::ExtensionHost` (the Resource version) lives behind path disambiguation.
